Who We Are

Born from a Passion for Perfumery

AZIMI was founded by Maryam Azimi with a passion for modern perfumery and elegant fragrance design. What began as a personal creative journey evolved into a refined perfume collection inspired by individuality, confidence, and timeless style.

Every bottle is a masterpiece — a story told through scent, a moment captured in glass. We believe fragrance is not merely a product, but an extension of one's identity.
